Carpool Etiquette 101: A Guide for Beginners

March 15, 2026 • 2 min read

Sharing a ride means sharing a space. When participating in the SaathChalo community, small behaviors go a long way in making the commute pleasant for everyone. Here’s a quick guide to keeping your rating golden.

For the Passengers

  • Punctuality is King: A driver's time is valuable. Aim to be at the pickup spot 2 minutes early. If you are going to be late, shoot them a quick WhatsApp message. Delaying the ride delays everyone else in the carpool.
  • Respect the Preferences: If the driver marked "No Music" or "No Smoking", adhere to it. The rules of the car are determined by the owner.
  • Payment Readiness: Have the exact fare ready in cash, or ask for their EasyPaisa details early in the ride. Don't cause a scene searching for change at the final drop-off.

For the Drivers

  • A Clean Ride: Ensure your car is tidy. You don't need a professional detail service daily, but removing trash and ensuring the seats are clean is required.
  • Drive Safely: You are responsible for other lives. No harsh braking, speeding, or texting while driving. Your reviews will quickly plummet if you make passengers uncomfortable.
  • Communication: If traffic is heavy on Shahrah-e-Faisal and you are 5 minutes late to the pickup, use the in-app chat or WhatsApp to inform the passenger. Silence causes unnecessary anxiety.

Follow these simple steps and you’ll find that carpooling is an incredibly rewarding social and economic experience!
