How It Works

Three steps. Half the bill.

01
📍
Set Your Route
Enter your pickup area and destination. We cover all major Karachi zones — PECHS, DHA, Gulshan, Clifton, North Nazimabad, Korangi and more.
02
🤝
Match & Connect
See verified drivers going your exact route. Check timing, car, cost share, and driver rating. Request to join and connect instantly.
03
⛽
Split & Ride
Cost split fairly by distance and passenger count at today's petrol rate. Coordinate on WhatsApp and pay directly in cash or EasyPaisa.
